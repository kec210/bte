Welcome to the U

Life at the University of Miami is a mix of strong academics, school pride, and the energy of living in South Florida. Students get to experience a campus filled with palm trees, warm weather, and the green and orange spirit of the Hurricanes. Whether students are walking to class, studying with friends, or going to campus events, UM has a unique environment that feels both academic and exciting.

The University of Miami stands out because it gives students the feeling of a real college campus while also being close to everything Miami has to offer.
