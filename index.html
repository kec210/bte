Welcome to Life at UM

Life at the University of Miami combines a strong college campus experience with the energy of living in South Florida. Students are surrounded by palm trees, warm weather, school spirit, and many opportunities to get involved. Whether it is studying on campus, going to athletic events, joining organizations, or spending time with friends, UM offers a balance of academics and social life.
