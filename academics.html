Learning at the University of Miami

Academics are a major part of life at the University of Miami. Students can study subjects like business, communication, science, engineering, music, health, and technology. Classes often include group work, presentations, projects, and real-world examples that connect what students learn to future careers.

UM also has libraries, study spaces, professors, and academic resources that help students stay organized and succeed throughout the semester.
