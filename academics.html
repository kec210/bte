Classes and Campus Learning

Academics are a major part of life at the University of Miami. Students can study in many different fields, including business, communication, science, engineering, music, and health-related programs. Classes often connect learning to real careers, internships, group projects, and hands-on experiences. The campus also has libraries, study spaces, and academic resources that help students stay organized and successful.
