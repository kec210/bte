Getting Involved on Campus

Student life at UM includes much more than classes. Students can join clubs, attend campus events, participate in Greek life, go to football games, and support Miami Hurricanes athletics. These activities help students meet people, build friendships, and feel more connected to the university.

Because UM is located in Miami, students also have access to beaches, restaurants, concerts, sports, and cultural events outside of campus.
