Getting Involved at UM

Student activities are a big part of the UM experience. Students can attend campus events, join clubs, participate in Greek life, go to football games, and support Miami Hurricanes athletics. These activities help students meet new people and feel more connected to the university. UM’s location also gives students access to Miami’s culture, restaurants, beaches, and events outside of campus.
